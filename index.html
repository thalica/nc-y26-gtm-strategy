01 · Pursuing — the opportunity

Organizations in the North Cone that need AI they can own: run locally, on their own data, in places where the cloud is not a reliable option.

A category with no local reference

Desktop AI supercomputing is new in PE · EC · CO. Nobody owns the conversation yet — the first brand to explain it becomes the default answer.

Demand exists before we created it

6 units sold since launch with zero physical stock, zero landing page and zero paid media. Every sale was pulled, not pushed.

The channel is already moving

Tauret (CO) and Memory Kings (PE) already list ATOM online and sell it by dropshipping, ahead of a formal launch.

02 · Why — why this matters now

This is the first product we take to market as a purely AI product, and it is the start of a wider shift: moving North Cone's communication toward AI across the whole portfolio.

Reason 01

It opens our AI communication

ATOM is the first time we dedicate a full plan to a product that is exclusively AI. What we learn here — language, objections, formats — is what we will apply to the rest of the portfolio.

Reason 02

It is how we learn the market

Running it end to end tells us who buys AI in PE · EC · CO, what they ask for and what convinces them — knowledge we do not have today and cannot buy.

Reason 03

The demand already exists

Channels have sold ATOM without any campaign behind it. The demand is real; our purpose now is to increase it, not to prove it.

03 · Audience — who we are targeting

End users first, reached through the channel that already has their contact list. We do not sell — we equip the distributor's database and put the product in front of the person who will run it.

Primary

Enterprises with an internal IT team

Companies holding sensitive customer data and already spending on cloud processing — ISPs, services, finance. Telecable Iquitos and Banco Pichincha are the live examples.

Secondary

Entrepreneurs and AI builders

Smaller teams training or running their own models, invited through the same distributor webinars for the whole North Cone.

Route

Distributors as the door

Deltron (PE) · Tauret (CO) · XPC, Tecnomega, Intcomex (EC). Their client and vendor databases are how we reach the end user at scale.

06 · How — the strategic approach

Three principles, executed as the 3-step plan in the next section.

Principle 01

Explain before we sell

Own the category question — "local AI without the cloud" — in Spanish, on our own landing page and in earned press, so the buyer arrives already educated.

Principle 02

Borrow the channel's reach

Every webinar and mailing goes out through a distributor's own database. Their invitation converts; ours would be cold.

Principle 03

Convert with proof, not specs

Turn Telecable Iquitos into the regional reference, then run 1:1 sessions with the highest-intent leads and a results press piece in Q4.

05 · The proof we are building

Case study in progress — Telecable Iquitos (PE)

Our answer to "who else is doing this?". A Peruvian ISP running ATOM in the Amazon, where the cloud is the weak link — the most persuasive version of the category argument we could ask for.

Customer

Telecable Iquitos

  • Family-owned business group; ISP + IPTV since 2003
  • Operates across the Peruvian Amazon — Iquitos and other branches
  • Captive base of residential and corporate users, with its own internal IT department
Why ATOM

The buying argument, in their words

  • Reduce dependency on cloud-hosted CRM and data processing
  • Keep control of critical information and customer data locally
  • Ensure access to AI tools where connectivity is limited or unstable

Status and what comes next

Current status

Pilot stage — two initiatives in testing

  • AI security-camera monitoring, and AI information management / customer support
  • Two labs: Lima trains and develops the models, Iquitos runs local inference and real-connectivity testing
  • No direct financial benefit yet — the value today is capability-building and validation
Long-term interest

Where this could go

  • Exploring healthcare support tools for low-connectivity areas
  • Exploring education tools — Spanish/English for Awajún-speaking children
  • Open to a joint case study once the pilot hits its targets
Gap — no completion date has been given. The PM is still collecting data and is asking Telecable for more information. The pipeline assumes delivery in W39; the Q4 results press piece cannot be booked until that date is confirmed.

01 · Pursuing — the opportunity

A product that is already selling faster than we can restock it. Click any card to open it.

Sell-through is already proven

211 units sold to resellers. Every unit distributors held in Peru (100/100) and Ecuador (32/32) is gone. This is the one SKU where we are not arguing that demand exists.

The gap is the target, not the appetite

382 units ordered against a 660-unit H2 target — 58%. Closing the remaining 278 is about restocking and shelf presence, not about convincing anyone.

The channel content model works

Seven reseller posts produced 959K views for USD 100 in cash plus five IMC boosts. Nothing else in the portfolio returns at that rate.

02 · Why — why this matters now

This is the proof case for the whole North Cone model: resellers as co-creators, IMC as the amplifier, and the end user as the target.

It proves the co-creation model

We did not buy reach — we boosted the resellers' own content. That is the cheapest, most repeatable way we have found to reach the end user, and it is what we would apply to the rest of the portfolio.

The risk has flipped to supply

With distributor stock at zero in two markets, the constraint is no longer demand. Peru's next 100 units arrive W40 and Ecuador's 40 in W36 — until then there is nothing to sell.

Being listed is not being seen

OLED sells on the visible difference against IPS. Until the monitor is on display in an anchor store, we are asking buyers to take that difference on faith.

04 · How we execute

Build → Activate → Scale

Launched in W26 and running to year end. Build and Activate overlap deliberately; Scale is where the monitor moves from listing to display.

1
W32 – W37 · Build

Arm the base before spend starts

Same proof kit in every market, and commitment locked before money moves.

  • Equip every market with the same proof kit — specs and visuals
  • Lock distributor commitment early: brief clients on stock timing and support materials
  • Find the marketplaces where we can be available
2
W36 – W45 · Activate

Turn on every channel at once, don't stagger it

The resellers carry the message; IMC pays for the reach.

  • Paid media everywhere simultaneously — Meta and Google boosted with IMC budget
  • Convert resellers into co-creators: push client content through their own channels
3
W45 – W52 · Scale

Read the signal, don't guess

Turn proven sell-through into permanent shelf presence, then ride peak season.

  • Scout exhibition space in the anchor stores — PC Factory, Éxito — where OLED can be seen, not just listed
  • Identify the key partners to keep pushing through this model
  • Turn the best-performing store into a showcase — permanent display, not a one-off open box
  • Ride the peak-season calendar (Cyber Days, Black Friday, Christmas) into stores that don't carry it yet
